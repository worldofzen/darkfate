Game music lives here.

  Sanctuary of Fading Embers.mp3   title screen and the resting area (courtyard, corridor)
  The King's Last Vigil.mp3        the boss fight (starts when you step through the fog)

Both loop, and switching between them always crossfades smoothly.
To use different files, change TRACKS at the top of src/audio.js.
Volumes (music vs. wind vs. thunder) are balanced in MIX in the same file.

(The original lossless .wav files are kept in music_source/, which isn't published.)
